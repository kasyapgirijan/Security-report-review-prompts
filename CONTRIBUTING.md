# Contributing

Contributions are welcome.

## Adding or changing a prompt

- Keep prompts evidence-driven and technically scoped.
- Do not encourage invented facts, exploit paths, CVSS metrics or compliance mappings.
- Separate root-cause remediation from temporary mitigation and defence-in-depth.
- Prefer clear, vendor-neutral guidance unless a prompt is intentionally platform-specific.
- Preserve the three review levels: Analyst, Senior and Principal / Brutal.
- Include an explicit instruction to mark unsupported claims as unable to validate.

## Pull requests

Describe:

- The assessment type and review level
- The gap the change addresses
- Why the checks are technically justified
- Any standards or references used
